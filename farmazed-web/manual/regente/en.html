Before you start

Sign in on the login page with the demo regent's e-mail and the password you were given; passwords are not printed in this guide. The regent tour only exists in the demo account.

The language switch (ES · EN · FR · PT) is in the top bar: the whole back office and the "Help" link (which opens this guide) change to the language you pick.

The "Demo tour" panel

At the bottom right you will see a card with the three steps of the tour. It ticks off what you have done, highlights the step you are on, and the "Go to this step" button takes you to the right screen. Progress is derived from the data: there is nothing to save.

The "Reset demo account" button in the top bar wipes what you did (including the demo client's work) and puts everything back to the start. You will have to sign in again.

The three steps

Around "Producto Ejemplo 3" (phase 8) and the "Farmacia Ejemplo" lead.

Review the document for Producto Ejemplo 3

The client uploaded the qualitative-quantitative formula. Open the file from "My Inbox", find document 15.5 and approve it (✓) or reject it (✗) with a reason the client will see in their portal.

Next step: 2. Confirm the technical check (phase 8)

2

Confirm the technical check (phase 8)

"Producto Ejemplo 3" is in phase 8, review of digital documentation. In the "Phase 8 — Confirmations" card record your technical/matrices confirmation; the legal one is entered by the lawyer.

Next step: 3. Handle the "Farmacia Ejemplo" lead

3

Handle the "Farmacia Ejemplo" lead

Under "Leads" you will find the request from "Farmacia Ejemplo" (status New). Open it, change the status to Contacted or leave a note: this is how the CRM is worked before the admin invites the client to create their account.

✓ Tour complete: you have seen the essentials.

The 13 phases of a file

Every file moves through these phases; "My Inbox" shows where each of yours is. The highlighted one is the one you touch during the tour.

  1. 1First contact
  2. 2Preliminary information intake
  3. 3Type of sanitary registration and registration route
  4. 4Preparing and sending the quote
  5. 5Payment of procedure costs
  6. 6Opening of the internal file (CRM)
  7. 7Instructions to the client: required documentation
  8. 8Receipt and review of digital documentation (control point)in the tour
  9. 9Request and shipment of original documents
  10. 10Receipt and verification of originals (control point)
  11. 11Preparation of DNFD and IEA dossiers
  12. 12Filing with DNFD and IEA
  13. 13Post-filing follow-up and management

Good to know

  • When you reject a document, the reason you write is shown to the client in their portal: be specific about what must be corrected.
  • Phase 8 requires two confirmations: technical/matrices (yours) and legal (the lawyer's). The file does not move on until both are in.
  • Leads are requests from the public website form; changing their status or leaving notes is how they are worked before the admin invites the company to create its account.
  • The application and this guide exist in Spanish, English, French and Portuguese; pick the language in the top bar.
